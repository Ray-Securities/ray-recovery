Delete your data

Ray File Recovery 2.0 and later · Ray Securities

The short version

Ray has no account, and we keep no data about you on any server, so there is nothing of yours for us to delete there. What Ray stores is on your phone, and you can delete it yourself.

What Ray keeps inside the app

Settings › Delete Ray's data removes:

  • your history of saves, restores, clean-ups and backups
  • your theme choice, and whether you have seen the welcome tour
  • the contacts backups kept inside Ray
  • Ray's cache: thumbnails, resized pictures and contact files used when restoring

To do it:

  1. Open Ray and go to Settings.
  2. Tap Delete Ray's data, then Delete data.

Only a few technical settings stay, such as which permissions Ray has already asked for. They hold nothing about you. Uninstalling Ray deletes everything, including those.

Permissions you gave Ray stay until you change them. To take them back, open Android's Settings › Apps › Ray File Recovery › Permissions.

Files you saved

Copies you saved are in the “Ray Recovery” folders in Pictures and Music, and contacts backups are in Download/Ray Recovery. These are ordinary files that belong to you. Delete Ray's data doesn't touch them, and they stay after Ray is uninstalled so you don't lose them. Delete them with your gallery or file manager app if you no longer want them.

Version 1 of the app used folders named “Recovered Photos”, “Recovered Videos”, “Recovered Audios”, “Recovered Documents”, “Recovered WhatsApp” and “Recovered Status”, and it moved files into them instead of copying. Files in those folders may be your only copy, so check before you delete them.

Emails you sent us

If you emailed us, we have your email address, your message and anything you attached. Ask at hello@raysecurities.com and we will delete them.

Questions

Email hello@raysecurities.com. We hold no data about you beyond emails you sent, so we can't look anything else up, but we're happy to help.
