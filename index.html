Help

Answers to common questions about Ray File Recovery. Can't find yours? Email hello@raysecurities.com.

How do I get back a photo I just deleted?

On Android 11 and newer, open Ray and tap Review and restore on the Recover tab (or Recently deleted on the Clean tab). Tap an item to look at it and restore it, or long-press to select several, then tap Restore. Android puts them back in their original folders.

Why can't Ray find something I deleted?

Ray can restore only what is still in Android's trash, and only apps that use that trash put things there. Android deletes trashed items for good after about 30 days. Some apps delete files right away, and Google Photos, Files by Google and many gallery apps keep their own trash instead, so check those too; Ray links to them under Other places to check.

Also:

  • Android 10 and older have no system trash.
  • If you let Ray see selected photos and videos only, items in the trash stay hidden. Tap Change selection to allow more.
  • Ray works with photos, videos and audio, not documents or other files.

Once a file is deleted for good, no app can bring it back.

Where do saved copies go?

When you tap Save copy, Ray puts the copy in a “Ray Recovery” album in Pictures (in Music for audio). The original stays where it was. You'll find saved copies in the Library tab too.

Version 1 of the app moved files into folders named “Recovered Photos”, “Recovered Videos” and similar instead of copying them, so files there may be your only copy.

Is it safe to remove files from the Clean tab?

Ray always asks first and shows what will be removed. On Android 11 and newer, anything you remove goes to Android's trash, so you can tap Undo right away or restore it for about 30 days. On Android 10 and older there is no trash, so removed files are deleted for good.

Duplicate photos are matched by size, resolution and contents, so only exact copies are found. The oldest photo in each group is always kept, and Ray checks the files again just before it removes anything.

How do I move my contacts to a new phone?

On the Recover tab, tap Back up contacts, then Back up now. Share the backup, or copy the Download/Ray Recovery folder, to the new phone. There, open Ray, tap Back up contacts and then Restore from a file, or open the .vcf file with the Contacts app. Contact photos and groups aren't included in the backup.

Can Ray recover WhatsApp messages?

No. Ray shows the photos and videos that chat apps saved to your phone (voice notes are under Audio), but chat messages are stored by the chat app itself and can only be restored from that app's own backup.

Does Ray upload my files?

No. Ray has no account, no ads and no servers, and it sends nothing off your phone by itself. A file leaves your phone only when you share it. Read the privacy policy for details.

How do I delete what Ray keeps?

Go to Settings › Delete Ray's data. Delete your data explains what it removes and what stays.
