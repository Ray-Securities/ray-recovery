The short version

Ray File Recovery is free to use, with no ads and no account. It can restore only what is still in Android's trash. We can't promise that any particular file can be recovered, so keep your own backups of anything important.

1. What Ray does

Ray lists the photos, videos and audio in Android's media library, shows what apps have moved to Android's trash and restores it, saves copies, helps you remove large files, duplicate photos and old screenshots, and makes contacts backups.

2. What Ray can't do

  • On Android 11 and newer, media that an app moves to Android's trash stays there for about 30 days, and then Android deletes it for good. Ray can restore items only while they are still in that trash.
  • Many apps delete files right away or keep their own trash instead. Ray can't see inside another app's trash or backup, so check that app too.
  • Android 10 and older have no system trash, so Ray can't restore files deleted there.
  • Files deleted for good and chat messages cannot be recovered by Ray.

3. Your choices and your files

Ray asks before it moves your photos, videos or audio to the trash or deletes them, and shows what will happen. On Android 10 and older, removing a file deletes it for good, and Ray warns you first. You are responsible for the files you choose to remove, share or keep. Restoring contacts is done by your Contacts app, which asks you first.
